## Access & Preparation

Carpets and upholstery must be ready for work on arrival.
If customers want items moved, they must move them themselves before the appointment.

## Water & Power

Customers must provide:

 - A working electric point

 - Access to water

## Quotes & Pricing

 - All pricing is confirmed after an initial phone call.

 - No hidden fees — ever.

 - Eligibility for discounts is discussed during booking.
   - Blue Light Card holders receive 10% off.
   - Veterans receive 20% off. 

## Payments

 - First‑time customers: pay half upfront and half on completion.

 - Returning customers: standard payment on completion unless otherwise agreed.
